Competitor Brief

We research your competitors, so your team doesn't have to guess.

Competitor Brief is a research service with an app. We study your market and your competitors, then give your whole team the answers in one simple app.

  1. You tell us about your companyWhat you sell, to whom, and who you think you compete with.
  2. We do the researchWe map your market and every competitor. As DevOps engineers we also install competitors' products in a live lab and test what they really do, not what their website says.
  3. You get the appAll our findings in one place: answers, battlecards and clear recommendations for leadership, marketing and sales.

In this demo you can preview step 3 right now. Describe any company and an AI builds a quick first draft from public information in about 30 seconds. The real app is built from our research.
