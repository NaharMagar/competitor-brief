## What they are
A workflow tool that automates change advisory board approvals and risk scoring inside
ServiceNow. Loved by IT governance and GRC teams.

## Strengths (concede)
- Deep ServiceNow integration; GRC and ITIL teams trust it.
- Mature risk-scoring questionnaires and CAB calendars.

## Weaknesses
- **Doesn't deploy or verify anything**: it records approvals; it can't tell whether the change
  actually worked or roll it back.
- Slows engineers down: CAB-style approvals don't fit 50+ deploys a day.
- No DORA, no telemetry.

## How to win
- Show ShipSafe Guard's policy-as-code: low-risk changes auto-approved with evidence; high-risk changes routed to ServiceNow approval. Faster *and* more auditable.
- Often a **complement** in very ITIL-heavy banks: ShipSafe posts evidence to the ChangeDesk/ServiceNow change record.

## Landmine questions
1. "After a change is approved, how do you prove it didn't harm production?"
2. "How many changes a day can your CAB realistically review?"
