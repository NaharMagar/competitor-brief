## Top win reasons (from interviews)
1. Statistical verification quality ("it caught what our thresholds missed") - 61% of wins.
2. Multi-runtime coverage (ECS/Lambda/VMs) - 44%.
3. Audit evidence pack - 39% (78% in regulated industries).
4. Per-service pricing with unlimited users - 33%.
5. Speed of POV and SE quality - 29%.

## Top loss reasons
1. **Price / budget** - 31% (mostly vs OpenDeploy OSS and KubeRoll in SaaS under 300 engineers).
2. **Incumbent bundle** - 24% (MegaSuite "already paid for").
3. **No decision / build** - 22% (DIY with Argo Rollouts; champion left; priorities shifted).
4. **Feature gaps** - 14% (feature flags/experimentation, Windows VMs, FedRAMP, mobile).
5. Other - 9%.

## What changed what we do
- Deals that ran the "bad deploy" side-by-side test vs MegaSuite: win rate 63% (vs 38% without).
- Leading with per-service pricing in the first call vs OpenDeploy: +11 pts win rate.
- Including the CISO by stage 2 in regulated deals: +14 pts win rate and +22% ACV.
- AutoShip AI: losses concentrated in companies < 300 engineers with AI-first CTOs; reply with
  reproducibility + compliance landmines early.
