# Market Overview: Deployment Safety / Progressive Delivery
*Fictional demo data · Competitor Brief. Market figures from the (fictional) Gridline Research 2026 report.*

## Size
- Progressive delivery & release verification market: **$2.4B (2026) → $6.1B (2030)**, ~26% CAGR.
- ShipSafe SAM (companies with 200+ engineers on cloud-native runtimes, US + Europe): ~14,000 companies, ~$1.9B.
- ShipSafe share of SAM today: ~2% ($41M ARR) - lots of headroom.

## Trends driving demand
1. **AI-generated code** roughly doubles change volume; change failure rate rises unless verification is automated.
2. **Platform engineering** teams standardize the "golden path" and want buy-over-build for deployment.
3. **Regulation**: DORA (EU Digital Operational Resilience Act) for financial entities from 2025, SOX, PCI DSS 4.0, HIPAA updates all demand provable change control.
4. **Multi-runtime reality**: average enterprise customer deploys to 2.7 runtimes (K8s + ECS/Lambda/VMs).
5. **Agents in operations**: engineering AI agents need a safe way to ship and check release health (ShipSafe MCP server).
